Business Implication — UAE Market Context
The 83.72% RERA price premium is the single most actionable finding in this project. For developers and agencies operating in Abu Dhabi (3% compliance), Sharjah (26%), RAK and Ajman (0%), pursuing RERA registration before listing is the highest-ROI compliance decision available. Dubai's 100% mandate serves as the benchmark — emirates that adopt similar frameworks will unlock significant pricing power for their property markets.

The +4.17% MoM price increase in March 2024 and the 268% 3BR-vs-studio gap are the two most strategically significant signals — the first confirms market resilience; the second confirms that family-sized units remain the UAE's premium value-capture vehicle. Developers should weight pipeline toward 3BR product to maximise margin.
