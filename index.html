Impact of Brexit on UK University Enrolment

Analysis of how Brexit reshaped international student demographics across all UK higher education institutions — tracking EU and non-EU enrolment from 2012 to 2023 using official HESA data. Quantifies the 2021 fee-change cliff edge (−51% EU enrolment in one year), identifies the pre-referendum shadow effect beginning in 2017, and conducts regional and institutional breakdowns.

How I Work

The pipeline,
start to finish

01

Understand the problem

Every project begins with the business question, not the data. What decision needs to be made? What does "good analysis" look like to the stakeholder? This shapes everything that follows.

02

Build a clean foundation

Raw data is messy. I invest time in inspection, type casting, null handling, and feature engineering — because insight built on weak data is not insight. Every pipeline is documented and reproducible.

03

Communicate with precision

Analysis that cannot be understood cannot be acted upon. Dashboards are designed for the decision-maker, not the analyst. Findings are written as conclusions, not observations.
